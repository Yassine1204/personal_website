**What should be on the website:**

1. Professional Bio: Introduce yourself on the homepage with a brief summary of who you are, your degree, and what you are currently working on. Include a friendly, clear headshot so people can put a face to your name.

2. Contact Information: Make it easy for potential employers or collaborators to reach out by using a simple contact form or listing your professional email.

3. Curriculum Vitae (CV) or Resume: Display your education, awards, and work experience. It is also highly recommended to provide a downloadable version so recruiters can easily save your resume.

4. Portfolio and Projects: Show off your skills beyond a simple list by including screenshots, videos, or links to personal projects. This could be design work, coding repositories, or research write-ups.

5. Extracurriculars and Training: Highlight any summer jobs, relevant internships, certifications, or workshops you have attended.

6. Blog or Industry News (Optional): Many experts on RippleMatch suggest sharing blog posts or relevant industry news to show you are willing to continue to learn and grow outside of the classroom.
